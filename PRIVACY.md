# Privacy Policy for Tabs Combiner

**Last updated:** [Date]

## Introduction

Tabs Combiner ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension handles your data.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing information.**

Tabs Combiner operates entirely locally on your device. All data is stored in your browser's local storage and never leaves your computer.

## Data Storage

The extension stores the following information locally in your browser:
- Tab group configurations
- Domain groupings you create
- Extension preferences

This data is stored using Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API and remains on your device. We have no access to this information.

## Data Usage

The data stored locally is used solely to:
- Remember your tab group configurations
- Restore your preferences when you use the extension
- Enable the tab management features

## Third-Party Services

Tabs Combiner does not use any third-party services, analytics, tracking, or advertising.

## Permissions

The extension requests the following permissions:

- **activeTab**: Allows access to the currently active tab to display its domain
- **scripting**: Enables the extension to interact with tabs
- **storage**: Stores your preferences and group configurations locally
- **tabs**: Allows querying and managing browser tabs

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data outside your browser.

## Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the extension or on our website.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the extension's support channels.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your locally stored data (through Chrome's extension storage viewer)
- Delete your data (uninstalling the extension removes all stored data)
- Export your data (group configurations can be exported from the options page)

## Compliance

This extension complies with:
- Chrome Web Store policies
- GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) requirements
- CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act) requirements
